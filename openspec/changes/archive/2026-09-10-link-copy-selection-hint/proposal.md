## Why

Link selection currently gives no indication that the next key will copy a target.

## What Changes

Show a localized Sage-equivalent hint in the existing gap below the composer while link selection is armed. Explain that the character after `~` copies its link or path and Esc cancels.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `quick-link-copy`: visible selection guidance.

## Impact

Shared main-pane rendering and English/Chinese catalogs only; bindings and layout height remain unchanged.
